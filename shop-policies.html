Shipping & returns

Shipping in the United States

Standard U.S. shipping for CCW Denver Ball Markers is $3, regardless of marker quantity. The India Cricket Custom Headcover ships free when ordered on its own. Applicable sales tax is shown at checkout. We do not offer international shipping at launch.

Items shipped by CCW

Orders ship the same or next business day. Expected U.S. delivery is 3–5 business days after ordering. The delivery window includes order processing and carrier transit. If we cannot meet the promised shipment date, we will contact you about the delay and your cancellation/refund options. Contact us if delivery is delayed.

Pins and Aces shipping

Pins and Aces ships its products directly. Available shipping rates appear at checkout. If your order also includes CCW gear, the items may arrive separately and supplier shipping may be added to the CCW rate.

Returns

For an unused item shipped by CCW, contact info@tcclubworks.io within 14 days of delivery to request a return. The buyer pays return postage for change-of-mind returns on these items.

For Pins and Aces items, email info@tcclubworks.io within 30 days of delivery to request a return. There is no return label fee or restocking fee. Please wait for return instructions before mailing an item.

We will review the returned item and contact you about the refund. Approved refunds go to the original payment method. For products with shipping included in the listed price, an accepted full return refunds that price without an outbound shipping deduction. Your payment provider may take additional time to post the credit.

Damaged, defective or incorrect items

Email us with your order number and a description of the problem. Photos can help us resolve it. CCW will work with you to resolve defects or an incorrect shipment, including return postage when needed. The change-of-mind return policy does not limit rights provided by applicable law.

Personalized work & cancellations

This store's return policy covers ready-made products. Any different terms for commissioned or personalized work will be disclosed and agreed before purchase. Contact us promptly if you need to cancel; we will confirm whether your order has shipped.
